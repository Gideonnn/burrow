Evenementen in Hedwig

Concerts, theatre, festivals and parties, booked half a year ahead through ticket shops whose mail never names the night the way you remember it. Evenementen is one page in Hedwig, under Overal, where Gideon and Eva keep every outing together: the date and the place, the tickets with their QR codes, what still has to happen before the night, the mails that belong to it, and every name it can be found by.

Notes. Evenementen sits under Overal because events are not per mailbox: both adults see the same list, and the toolbar search on this page searches events by every alias (try 90s party; apostrophes and accents do not matter). The sidebar badge counts what needs one of them within 30 days. Rows carry three quiet facts: tickets, the personalisation deadline (amber inside 30 days) and whether it is in Wij. Every field in the detail is plain text until you hover it, like Finder's Get Info; a change is saved at once, written to Wie deed wat, and, once calendar sync exists, pushed to Wij. The checklist and ticket rows are the Evenement card's, so a ticked item in the card is ticked here.

2Elke staat

The top of the detail in each state, side by side. These are live: ticking an item here ticks it in the window above, and Open selects the event there.

3Kaarten tonen

At the door you want one thing: a QR code a scanner can read. Kaarten tonen (key K) opens a full-screen view on black, raises the screen to full brightness while it is open and puts it back after.

How it behaves

  • One ticket at a time, large; arrows or swipe for the next one. The holder's name is printed under the code because a personalised ticket is checked against an ID.
  • Opens on the first ticket of the person whose Mac it is, so Gideon and Eva can each show their own from their own device.
  • Works offline: the pdf, the Wallet pass or the screenshot is cached on the Mac once the event is less than a week away.
  • A Wallet pass also offers Voeg toe aan Wallet, for the phone at the door.
  • The QR is drawn from the stored file, never re-encoded, and never leaves Dobby and the Macs: no cloud AI, and Telegram gets only "Je kaarten staan in Hedwig".

4Nieuw evenement, of maak er een van een mail

An order mail that Hedwig recognises with certainty (schema.org EventReservation or a known shop template) becomes an event on its own, as Neon Nineties did. Anything else goes through this sheet: from the mail's menu or its card (Maak evenement van deze mail), prefilled and marked per field, or empty from Nieuw evenement. Every field stays editable.

Sheet

What the sheet does

  • Fields read from the mail say uit de mail; a field it could not read stays empty and is marked as missing (niet in de mail, ontbreekt). Nothing is guessed.
  • Winky reads it, locally on Dobby, because the mail holds the tickets. A cloud bot never sees this sheet's input.
  • Ook uit de mail lists what comes along: the ticket files, a deadline sentence turned into a checklist item with its two reminders, and the mail itself as the first linked mail.
  • Aliases start with title, kind, venue, organiser, shop and order number; remove what is noise, add what you would type.
  • Meteen in Wij zetten is on by default because every event is shared.

5Gedrag

The rules behind the page, short.

Mails join by themselves

A later mail joins an event by its order number, or by event name plus date. A weaker guess (only the organiser) shows on the mail as Hoort dit bij Neon Nineties? with Ja and Nee; nothing is linked silently on a guess. Koppel een mail does it by hand, and a linked mail gets the event's aliases as search words.

Deadlines and reminders

A deadline sentence in a mail ("uiterlijk 14 dagen voor het evenement op naam") becomes a checklist item with a date. Four weeks and one week before, it appears in Vandaag and as a notice to both. Whoever ticks it closes it for both. Custom items have an optional date and person; only dated ones remind.

Two people, one event

Both can change everything. Each change is saved per field with who and when, shown in Wie deed wat. The other person gets a quiet notice only for changes that matter on the night: date, times, venue, tickets, and ticked deadlines. Last write wins per field; there is no locking.

Tickets are sensitive

A QR code is money. Ticket files live in the platform's uploads on Dobby and in an offline cache on the Macs; QR codes in screenshots are read on the Mac (Vision), not by a model. No cloud AI sees them, Kreacher's tools cannot reach them, Telegram gets a pointer only.

Wij

In Wij zetten creates the event in the shared Google calendar with the times, the address, a link back to Hedwig and the tickets as attachments for whoever opens it on the phone. Once sync exists, a time moved in Wij shows up here as a change by that person.

After the night

The day after, the event moves under Geweest. Notes, mails and aliases stay, so Jazz in het park still finds last year's order. The ticket files are removed from Dobby 30 days after the event.

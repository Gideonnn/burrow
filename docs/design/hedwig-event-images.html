Evenementen met een afbeelding

Builds on evenementen v2. Each outing gets one picture, taken from the order mail or the event's website, so you recognise it at a glance. The picture only decorates: the one status sentence, the three facts and Details stay as in v2. Nothing to set up; changing the picture lives in Details, and pasting (⌘V) or dropping a picture on the top of the page sets it too.

22 October 2026·Invented events, shops and venues; the pictures are drawn stand-ins

0Rules

Uit de mail

The image in the mail's event data, otherwise the largest real image in the mail. Never logos, icons, dividers or tracking pixels.

Van de website

No usable image in the mail: the og:image of the event page the mail links to.

Anders kleuren

Nothing found: a calm band in colours derived from the title, the same every time. Never a stock photo.

Eén keer opgehaald

The server downloads the image once and keeps it with the tickets. The Mac only loads it from Burrow, so the sender sees nothing when you open the page.

Click the outings: Neon Nineties has a big mail image, Zomerweide one from the website, Theo Voorbeeld a small square poster, Stille Wateren a small wide banner, Foute Vrijdag XL nothing. Notes. The list stays a list: a 44 px thumbnail at the end of the row, or with Kleurstrook only a 5 px strip in the picture's main colour; the date tile and the status sentence keep the left side. Past outings turn grey. The picture is 210 px high at the top of the page and scrolls away with it; the title sits on a dark scrim (a gradient behind the text). Dropping an image anywhere on the page still adds a ticket (screenshots are tickets), so your own picture goes onto the top of the page, the drop area under Details or Andere afbeelding. Pasting (⌘V) a picture or a link copied in Safari or a mail works in the same places. Without a picture the list shows a calm tile and the page a compact band, both in the title's colours.

In this order

  1. De mailThe image in the schema.org event data; otherwise the largest image of at least 400 × 200 px that is not narrower than 1:5 and not named or linked like a logo or icon, and not from a known tracker.
  2. De websiteThe og:image (the picture a site offers for link previews) of the event page in the mail or in its event data.
  3. KleurenTwo colours from the title, dark enough for white text, with a few soft rings.

How it is kept

  • The server fetches once, without cookies, when the outing is made, scales to at most 1600 px wide and stores it next to the tickets, with its main colour for the strip.
  • A doubtful mail (the card with Bewaar) fetches nothing until you press Bewaar.
  • Never stretched: a picture under 1000 px wide, or squarer than 4:3, is shown sharp at its own size on a blurred copy of itself (section 5).

4Licht en donker

The text on the picture is white in both modes. In dark mode the picture is dimmed to 86% so it does not glare next to the dark page, and the date tile turns dark.

The scrim only darkens the lower half, so the top of the picture keeps its colour. The colour bands are made at 20 to 30% lightness, so they pass even without a scrim.

5Lastige afbeeldingen

Order mails rarely have a perfect 3.4:1 banner. Hedwig fills the band only with a picture that is big and wide enough; anything else is shown sharp at its own size, on a blurred and darkened copy of itself.

Besloten: de miniatuur aan het eind van de rij, die herken je sneller. Een evenement zonder afbeelding krijgt een rustig tegeltje in de kleuren bij de titel.

BNiets gevonden: de rustige kleurband

Besloten: zonder afbeelding krijgt de pagina een lage band in kleuren bij de titel, ook na Geen afbeelding. Zo ogen de pagina's gelijk.

CAfbeeldingen van buiten: ja

Besloten: de server haalt afbeeldingen uit de mail en van de website op en bewaart een kopie. De Mac laadt ze alleen van Burrow.

Agenda's in Burrow

Calendars come in with the mail account, are kept on Dobby, and tell each person what the other added to a shared calendar. Bots can add events too. The plan, with the limits per provider, is docs/plans/2026-10-22-calendars.md.

22 October 2026·Invented people: Gideon and Eva share the calendar Wij

1Koppelen

After the provider's login, Hedwig asks what to sync, as Apple's Internet Accounts does. Notes are not on the list. Calendars shared with you, such as Wij, are listed with who shares them; Burrow keeps one copy however many logins see it.

Notes. Google and Microsoft ask for the extra rights in the same login. Fastmail needs a second field (an app password for the calendar) when Agenda is on. A mailbox that is already connected shows Agenda en Contacten toevoegen in Instellingen, which runs the same login once more.

2Meldingen

When Eva adds something to Wij, Gideon gets a notice, and the other way round; never about your own change. Additions carry the name. A change made outside Burrow has no name, because Google does not say who made it; changes through Burrow do.

Rules

  • Bundled per two minutes, per calendar switchable, quiet hours respected.
  • Only time, place and cancellation changes notify, so an unnamed change seldom reaches the one who made it.
  • New items from someone else stay in Vandaag with Nieuw until you have seen them, also when they are not today.
  • Telegram (Patronus) is a third channel once it exists.

3Via een bot

From Telegram, Claude or Codex: a bot may add and change events in the calendars its key may write. It never invites guests and never deletes what it did not make without your click, and everything it does can be undone for a day.

Rights

  • New key rights calendar and calendar-write, per calendar like mailboxes.
  • Tools: list_events, free_busy, add_event, update_event, delete_event.
  • Guests and deleting someone else's event become a task that waits for your click, as sending mail does.
